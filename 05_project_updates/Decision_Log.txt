## Decision #11 – Invoice Print Optimization
- **Date:** 18 August 2026
- **Decision:** Remove unsupported `min-height: auto` from POS print stylesheet and replace it with cross-browser compatible `min-height: 0`.
- **Rationale:** Ensures that the printed bills don't break across older versions of browsers and correctly resets minimum constraints during physical/PDF printing.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

---

## Decision #12 – Forecasting Multi-Model Tournament & Classification Seeding
- **Date:** 18 August 2026
- **Decision:** Implement Syntetos-Boylan Classification (SBC) using ADI and $CV^2$ to categorize demand patterns, and execute a tournament between Prophet, Holt-Winters, and Exponential Smoothing. Seed the database with 18 months of mock history to test the pattern classes.
- **Rationale:** Aligns with findings from the Springer 2026 base paper that different patterns require different forecasting methods to achieve optimal accuracy.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

---

## Decision #14 – Kaggle Real Retail Dataset Integration & Product Name Mapping
- **Date:** 3 September 2026
- **Decision:** Ingest 18,260 real daily transactions from the Kaggle Store Item Demand dataset into SQLite and map numeric IDs to realistic grocery catalog items (Milk, Bread, Rice, Eggs, Olive Oil, Coffee, etc.).
- **Validation:** Prophet outperformed Holt-Winters and Exponential Smoothing on real data (e.g. Milk MAE: Prophet 5.08 vs HW 5.75 vs SES 6.64).
- **Rationale:** Resolves dataset availability concerns while preserving the exact ERP project scope and architecture.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED

## Decision #15 – Master Unified Top 30 Grocery Dataset Creation
- **Date:** 3 September 2026

## Decision #16 – Small Store Restocking Math & Master Top 30 Self-Train Endpoint Sync
- **Date:** 3 September 2026
- **Decision:** Updated inventory restock formula to a realistic 7-day store planning cycle (`target_inventory = 7 * avg_daily_demand + safety_stock`), yielding realistic 2-to-3 digit restocking order volumes (e.g. 105 to 398 units instead of 5,740 units). Synced `/api/forecasting/train` endpoint to load `master_grocery_top30.xlsx` on retrain events.
- **Rationale:** Ensures website self-train actions preserve the 30 Top Grocery Catalog items and generate realistic retail order volumes for review demos.
