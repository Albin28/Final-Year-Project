GUIDELINES FOR SEMINAR and SEMINAR  PRESENTATION  
 
1. Topic Selection 
● Core Relevance: The selected topic must be within the core areas of Computer Science 
and Engineering. Choose a current, broad-based topic rather than a very specific research 
work. 
● Project Linkage: The seminar topic should be closely linked to the final year project 
area.  
2. Source, Extended Abstract and Approval 
● Reputed Sources: Base the seminar topic on reputed and indexed international journal 
papers (years 2022-2026). 
o Example Journal: Machine Learning, Impact Factor 3.641 (Web of Science)- H 
index 155 (Google) -Impact Factor 7.105 (Scopus). 
o IEEE Xplore Digital Library, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect,SpringerLink 
● Extended abstract: Submit an extended abstract within 500-600 words. The abstract 
should include:  
(i) Relevance of the topic in the present scenario. 
(ii) Works carried out. 
(iii) Brief of the results. 
● Abstract Submission: Submit hard copies of the abstract and base paper to the Guide for 
approval immediately after topic finalization. Also upload the same in the drive shared 
and paste the link to the file in the google sheet shared. 
● PowerPoint Slides: Get the seminar PowerPoint slides approved by the Guide at least 
three working days before the seminar. 
● Base paper Submission: Submit a report to the panel at the time of presentation, which 
includes the base paper and the front page of the PPT signed/verified by the guide. 
3. Seminar Presentation 
● Time Allocation: 
o Presentation: 15 minutes. 
o Q&A, Discussion: 10 minutes. 
● Slide Limit: Presentation slides should be limited to 18-20 slides.

Bullet Points: Use bullet points instead of paragraphs. 
● 
Visual Aids: Include charts, graphs, and images to illustrate points. 
● 
Font Size: Ensure the text is readable. 
● 
Consistency: Maintain a consistent style and format throughout the slides. 
●

4. Post-Presentation 
● Draft Report: Submit a hard copy of the draft seminar report in LaTeX format for 
approval to Guide within seven days after the seminar. 
● Final Report: Submit the completed seminar report and evaluated draft copy within two 
weeks after the seminar to Guide and then to the Seminar coordinator. 
5. Seminar Diary 
● Maintain a detailed seminar log book that records all aspects of your seminar journey, 
including topic selection, research progress, feedback, and questions asked during the 
seminar(Template). 
● Hold weekly meetings with your Guide to review and update your seminar diary. The 
diary should be submitted to the seminar coordinator for verification when asked for.  
6. Evaluation 
● Panel: The seminar presentation will be evaluated by a panel(IEC) consisting of the 
academic coordinator, seminar coordinators, guide, and subject expert. 
● Knowledge Check: If the panel feels that the student lacks sufficient knowledge, they 
may be asked to repeat the seminar. 
● Questions: Students are encouraged to ask questions during the presentation. 
● Language: The seminar should be conducted in English without the use of mobile 
phones or paper/books. 
● Uniform: Students must be in full uniform, including an ID card. 
● Mark distribution: 
Seminar Guide: 20 marks (Background Knowledge – 10 (The guide shall give deserving 
marks for a candidate based on the candidate's background knowledge about the topic 
selected), Relevance of the paper/topic selected – 10). 
Seminar Coordinator: 20 marks (Seminar Diary – 10 (Each student shall maintain a 
seminar diary and the guide shall monitor the progress of the seminar work on a weekly 
basis and shall approve the entries in the seminar diary during the weekly meeting with the 
student), Attendance – 10). 
Presentation: 40 marks to be awarded by the IEC (Clarity of presentation – 10, 
Interactions – 10 (to be based on the candidate's ability to answer questions during the 
interactive session of her/his presentation), Overall participation – 10 (to be given based 
on her/his involvement during interactive sessions of presentations by other students), 
Quality of the slides – 10). 
Report: 20 marks to be awarded by the IEC (check for technical content, overall quality, 
templates followed, adequacy of references, etc.).
