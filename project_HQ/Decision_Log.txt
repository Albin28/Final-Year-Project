## Decision #13 – Forecasting Multi-Model Tournament & Classification Seeding
- **Date:** 18 August 2026
- **Decision:** Implement Syntetos-Boylan Classification (SBC) using ADI and $CV^2$ to categorize demand patterns, and execute a tournament between Prophet, Holt-Winters, and Exponential Smoothing. Seed the database with 18 months of mock history to test the pattern classes.
- **Rationale:** Aligns with findings from the Springer 2026 base paper that different patterns require different forecasting methods to achieve optimal accuracy.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED

## Decision #18 – Advanced ML Benchmarking (Tree Regressors), Feature Importance & ABC-XYZ Inventory Matrix
- **Date:** 18 September 2026 – 19 September 2026
- **Decision:** Elevate the AI core beyond basic time-series models by introducing:
  1. **Tree-Based ML Regressors (Random Forest & Gradient Boosting)** with engineered lag features (`t-1`, `t-7`, 30-day rolling mean/std, day-of-week, seasonality).
  2. **Explainable AI (XAI) & Feature Importance**: Quantify and chart feature contributions for model explainability during academic evaluation.
  3. **Dedicated ML Model Leaderboard Dashboard**: Live model comparison (Random Forest vs Gradient Boosting vs Prophet vs Holt-Winters vs SES) with $R^2$, MAE, RMSE, MAPE metrics.
  4. **ABC-XYZ Inventory Matrix & Anomaly Detection**: K-Means clustering for inventory velocity/predictability categorization + Isolation Forest for stock anomaly detection.
- **Rationale:** Addresses academic evaluation requirements for model explainability, feature engineering rigor, and defensible ML methodologies while reinforcing ERP supply chain intelligence.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED

## Decision #19 – Continuous Prediction Tracking & Real-Time Drift Monitoring Engine
- **Date:** 18 October 2026
- **Decision:** Implemented an automated background tracking engine (`prediction_tracker.py`, `scheduler.py`) that matches daily forecasted sales with actual POS transaction checkout figures. Visualized continuous error progression in the frontend (`AccuracyTrendChart.jsx`) calculating running MAE, MAPE, and directional bias.
- **Rationale:** Ensures machine learning models do not operate in a black-box silo. Evaluators can verify model reliability over time and detect data drift or forecast degradation before inventory runs short.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED

---

## Decision #20 – Offline Pretraining Engine, Persistent Forecast Cache & Live Health Telemetry
- **Date:** 18 October 2026
- **Decision:** Engineered an offline pretraining service (`pretrain_all_products.py`) that fits tournament models for all 30 grocery SKUs and serializes 30-day forecasts, metrics, and winning model assignments into `forecast_cache.json`. Added a real-time status LED component (`ModelStatusLED.jsx`) in the header nav to communicate model health, training timestamp, and cache status to the user. Packaged one-click execution via `Launch_NexusERP.bat` and `Train_NexusERP_Models.bat`.
- **Rationale:** Solves cold-start latency (reducing dashboard SKU load times from >5s per model fit to <40ms), ensuring buttery smooth evaluator demos while providing visual confirmation of model health.
- **Status:** ✅ IMPLEMENTED & VERIFIED

## Decision #21 – Closed-Loop Purchase Order Lifecycle & Dynamic Restocking Workflow
